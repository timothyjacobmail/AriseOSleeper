Arise O Sleeper

A personal Android alarm that helps you wake up with verse checks and a spoken morning briefing.

Wake-up routines

Set daily alarm times and complete your chosen passage by typing or dictation. A routine can include several wake-up checks. After the final check, the optional morning briefing reads selected notification previews, calendar events and Google Tasks.

Morning briefing

  • WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business and Gmail notification previews, with filters for senders, conversations and message text.
  • Today’s and tomorrow’s events from calendars synced to your phone.
  • Incomplete tasks from your selected Google Tasks list, including the “Immediate tasks” list. You can include undated tasks or limit the briefing to tasks due today and tomorrow.
  • Read, replay and stop controls using Android text-to-speech.

Permissions and Google access

You choose whether to grant notification, calendar and microphone access. Google Tasks connects through Google account authorization with the read-only tasks.readonly scope. The app processes the briefing on your phone.

Read the privacy policy for details about local storage, Google access and speech providers.
