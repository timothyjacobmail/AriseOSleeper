Arise O Sleeper is a personal Android utility maintained by GitHub user timothyjacobmail. This page explains how the project is provided.

Using the app

The app requires a compatible Android device. Alarm delivery, calendar synchronization, notification previews and speech depend on Android permissions, device settings and installed services. Test an alarm and briefing on your own device when configuring them.

Optional Google features

Google Tasks is optional and requires authorization for your own Google account. Google’s services and account access are subject to Google’s applicable terms and policies. You can disconnect access through the app and your Google account settings.

Privacy and support

Review the privacy policy for information about permissions, storage and service providers. The project source and setup instructions are available on GitHub. Questions and issues can be submitted through the project’s issue tracker.
